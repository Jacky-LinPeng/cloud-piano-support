Help & SupportPrivacy Policy

Cloud Piano is a playful piano for iPhone and iPad. Follow the glowing keys, try a rhythm challenge, or make your own tune. Replay the notes you actually played.

Getting started

Turn your device sideways. Choose a song, listen to the demonstration, then follow the glowing key. Guided Play waits for the correct note. Free Play lets you make your own melody.

Your performances

The latest 20 performances are stored on this device. Open My Performances to listen again or delete a recording. The app saves key events, not microphone audio. Parents & Settings can clear all saved performances and progress.

Language and sound

Version 1.2.0 adds 16 interface languages. The app follows your preferred supported device language, or the app language selected in iOS Settings. If sound is quiet, check the device media volume and Piano Volume in the app settings.

Free play and advertisements

All songs, Free Play and playback work offline, with no account or in-app purchases. From version 1.1.0, banner and app-open ads appear at suitable times outside piano play and playback. After a performance you may choose to watch a rewarded ad for 30 minutes without banner or app-open ads. Ads require internet and may not be available. Watching is never required to use a music feature.

Contact

For help, report your device model, iOS version, app version and what happened. Do not include passwords, payment details or a child’s personal information.
